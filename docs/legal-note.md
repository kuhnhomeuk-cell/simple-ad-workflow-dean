# A note on reusing another advertiser's creative

Meta publishes the Ad Library so that ads can be looked at, and pulling a competitor's creative and copy from it for analysis is ordinary practice. Republishing a translated copy of that same photograph and that same ad copy as your own is a different thing. The copy is the original advertiser's written work and the photograph is theirs or their photographer's, and translating either does not change who owns it. Running it in a market they are not in does not change it either. Our recommendation is to treat what this pipeline produces as a brief rather than a press-ready asset: it tells you what the offer is, how the story is structured, which claims carry it and what the creative does, and you then shoot and write your own version from that. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice. The call is yours.
